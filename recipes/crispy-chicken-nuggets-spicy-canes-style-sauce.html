Crispy Chicken Nuggets with Spicy Cane's-Style Sauce

Ground chicken breast is seasoned, shaped into nuggets, and frozen, then dipped in a light batter and fried at 350°F until golden and crisp. Serve them hot with a spicy mayonnaise sauce flavored with sriracha and Worcestershire.

Ingredients

Chicken mixture

  • 1 lb ground chicken breast
  • 1 egg (large)
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp white pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder

Wet batter

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika or chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 cup cold water (to 3/4 cup)

Dry coating

  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour (plain flour, for coating)

For frying

  • neutral oil (about 1 1/2 inches deep in the pot)

Spicy Cane's-style sauce

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp sriracha (to 2 tbsp; start with 1 tbsp and add more to taste)
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper (freshly ground)

Steps

Shape and freeze

  1. Mix the ground chicken breast, egg, cornstarch, and seasonings until smooth and sticky. Lightly oil your hands and shape into small nuggets about 1/2 inch thick. Place them on a parchment-lined tray and freeze for a few hours, until frozen.

Batter

  1. Whisk together the flour, cornstarch, and seasonings for the wet batter. Start with 1/2 cup cold water and add more a little at a time until the batter coats the nuggets and the excess drips off easily.

Fry

  1. Heat the oil to 350°F. Lightly coat each frozen nugget in plain flour, shake off the excess, then dip it into the wet batter. Let the extra batter drip off before frying.
  2. Fry in small batches, turning occasionally, until golden and crispy. Start checking around 4–5 minutes and cook until the centers reach 165°F. Drain on a wire rack and let them rest for 1–2 minutes.

Sauce

  1. Mix the mayonnaise, sriracha, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, and black pepper. Start with 1 tablespoon sriracha and add more to taste. Serve with the hot nuggets for dipping.
