Homemade Broccoli Cheddar Soup

A one-pot broccoli cheddar soup starts with a butter, onion, and garlic roux, then simmers chopped broccoli and shredded carrots in chicken broth and half and half. Off the heat, freshly shredded sharp cheddar is stirred in until the soup is melted and creamy.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion (small; diced)
  • 2 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cup chicken broth
  • 2 cup half and half
  • 3 cup broccoli (fresh; chopped)
  • 1 cup carrot (shredded)
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 cup sharp cheddar cheese (freshly shredded)

Steps

Make the base

  1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened. Stir in the garlic, then sprinkle in the flour and whisk until combined.
  2. Slowly pour in the chicken broth and half and half, whisking until smooth. Add the broccoli, carrots, salt, pepper, and paprika. Simmer for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the broccoli is tender and the soup has thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and gradually stir in the freshly shredded cheddar until melted and creamy.
