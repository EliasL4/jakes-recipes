Creamy Tuscan Chicken Pasta

Seasoned chicken thighs are seared until golden, then sliced over penne in a Parmesan cream sauce with cherry tomatoes and spinach. The sauce is loosened with pasta water and finished with lemon juice.

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 2 chicken thigh (boneless, skinless)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter (divided)
  • 4 clove garlic (minced)
  • 1/3 cup cherry tomato (halved)
  • 1 1/2 tsp tomato purée
  • 3/4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp chili flakes
  • 1/3 cup chicken stock (low-sodium)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 1/2 cup baby spinach
  • 1/3 cup Parmesan (grated; plus more to finish)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 7 oz penne (200g; about 2 cups dry)

Steps

Sear the chicken

  1. Season the chicken thighs with the salt, black pepper, smoked paprika, and garlic powder. Sear in the olive oil and 1 tbsp butter over medium-high heat, 5 to 6 minutes per side, until golden and cooked to 165°F (74°C). Set aside to rest.

Cook the pasta

  1. Boil the penne in salted water 1 minute shy of the package directions. Save some pasta water before draining.

Make the sauce

  1. In the same pan, melt the other 1 tbsp butter and cook the garlic for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  2. Add the cherry tomatoes, tomato purée, Italian seasoning, and chili flakes. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, until the tomatoes soften and start to burst.
  3. Pour in the chicken stock, scrape up the browned bits, and simmer 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in the heavy cream and simmer gently. Add the spinach and stir until just wilted, then melt in the parmesan until the sauce is smooth and silky.

Finish

  1. Stir in the lemon juice, then toss the penne through the sauce with splashes of pasta water until glossy and coated.
  2. Slice the chicken, lay it over the top, finish with extra parmesan, and serve right away.
