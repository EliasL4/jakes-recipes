Minestrone Soup

A hearty Italian vegetable soup with onion, carrot, celery, beans, green beans, and small pasta simmered in an herb-flecked tomato broth. Finish each bowl with parsley, a pinch of red pepper flakes, and optional Parmesan.

Ingredients

Soup

  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion (medium; diced)
  • 2 carrot (medium; chopped)
  • 2 rib celery (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tsp sea salt (plus more to taste)
  • black pepper (freshly ground; several grinds, to taste)
  • 3 clove garlic (grated)
  • 1 can diced tomatoes (28-ounce)
  • 1 1/2 cup white beans or kidney beans (cooked; drained and rinsed)
  • 1 cup green beans (chopped)
  • 4 cup vegetable broth
  • 2 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 3/4 cup small pasta (elbows, shells, or orecchiette)
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley (chopped; for serving)
  • red pepper flakes (optional, for heat; amount not specified)
  • Parmesan cheese (grated; optional, for serving)

Steps

Cook the soup

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, celery, salt, and several grinds of black pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 8 minutes, until the vegetables begin to soften.
  2. Add the garlic, diced tomatoes, beans, green beans, vegetable broth, bay leaves, oregano, and thyme. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes.
  3. Stir in the pasta and cook uncovered for 10 more minutes, until the pasta is cooked through.
  4. Season to taste. Serve topped with parsley, red pepper flakes, and Parmesan if desired.

Notes

  • Best soon after cooking: the pasta keeps absorbing broth as leftovers sit.
  • To make ahead, cook the pasta separately and stir it into bowls when serving; or skip the pasta and add an extra cup of beans. Without pasta in the pot, the soup keeps up to 4 days in the fridge.
  • Source suggestions for swaps: fennel for celery; zucchini or yellow squash for (or with) green beans; fresh Roma tomatoes in summer; spinach or kale near the end; pearled farro or barley instead of pasta; homemade bean cooking liquid in place of some broth.
  • For a vegan bowl, skip the Parmesan (or use a vegan pesto garnish, as the source suggests).
  • Source lists a Dutch oven (Staub) under equipment; any large pot works.
